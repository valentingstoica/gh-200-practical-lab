How do steps within the same job run?	Sequentially, on the same runner.	JOB JOB-01 S5
How do jobs without dependencies run?	In parallel, each on a separate runner.	JOB JOB-02 S5
What does <code>needs: test</code> do?	The job waits for <code>test</code> and runs only if <code>test</code> succeeded.	JOB JOB-03 S5
If a job listed in <code>needs</code> fails, what happens to the dependent job by default?	It is skipped.	JOB JOB-04 S5
Where can you see the order of jobs defined by <code>needs</code>?	In the run graph in the GitHub Web UI.	JOB JOB-05 S5
How do you find why a job failed in the Web UI?	Open the run, select the failed job, expand the failed step and read its log (error message and exit code).	TRB TRB-02 S5
